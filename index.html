Privacy Policy for Our Games

Effective date: October 16, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains what information is collected when you play our games, how it is used, and the choices you have. It applies to all of our games, including the games we add in the future.

Our Games

This policy currently covers:

  1. Cosmic Bubbles — a bubble-shooter puzzle game for Android and iOS.

We plan to release more games. When we do, they will be listed here, and this policy will apply to them. If a future game collects information in a way that is not described below, we will update this policy before that game is released.

Information We Collect

We do not ask you to create an account, and we do not collect your name, email address, phone number, or precise location.

Stored only on your device

  • Game progress: unlocked levels, stars, best scores, and sound settings are saved locally on your device. This data is not sent to us. Uninstalling the game removes it.

Collected automatically by third-party services

Our games use the following services, which may collect information as described by their own policies:

  • Google AdMob (advertising). To show ads, AdMob may collect your device's advertising ID, IP address, device and operating-system information, and ad interaction data. Ads may be personalized or non-personalized depending on your device settings and region. Google's Privacy Policy · How Google uses data from apps
  • Firebase Analytics. Collects anonymous usage events (for example, a level started, a level cleared, or a game over), along with an app-instance identifier, device model, OS version, and approximate region. We use this to understand how the game is played and to improve it.
  • Firebase Crashlytics. Collects crash reports and diagnostic data (such as device model, OS version, and the state of the app when it crashed) so we can find and fix bugs.
  • Firebase Remote Config. Downloads game settings (such as ad frequency and difficulty tuning) from our servers. It may use an app-instance identifier to deliver those settings. Firebase Privacy and Security

How We Use Information

  • To operate and improve our games
  • To find and fix crashes and bugs
  • To balance game difficulty and tune features
  • To show ads, which help keep the games free

We do not sell your personal information.

Advertising Choices

  • Android: open Settings → Google → Ads to reset your advertising ID or opt out of ad personalization.
  • iOS: on first launch the game asks for permission to track you across other companies' apps and websites. You can change this at any time in Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.

Opting out does not remove ads. It only means they are less relevant to you.

Children's Privacy

Our games are not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, contact us at karamveersg1313@gmail.com and we will take steps to address it.

Data Retention

Analytics and crash data are retained by Firebase according to Google's standard retention periods. Game progress is stored on your device until you uninstall the game or clear its data.

Security

We rely on the security practices of Google and Firebase to protect data in transit and at rest. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

Your Rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, or delete personal information about you, or to object to certain processing. Because we do not hold account information about you, most of this data lives with the third-party services listed above, and you can manage it through your device's privacy settings. To make a request or ask a question, email us at karamveersg1313@gmail.com.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time, for example when we add a new game. The effective date at the top will change when we do. We encourage you to review this page periodically.
